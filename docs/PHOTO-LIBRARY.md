# Horsehead Experience photo library

The venue-supplied `Horsehead.zip` contains 104 photo files, 90 exact-unique photographs, and this build deliberately curates 81 individually selected photos for the public gallery.

The gallery features genuine venue images and separate categories: Landscape & lake, Ceremony spaces, Reception & gatherings, Real wedding moments, Stay & explore. Duplicate exports, very similar crops and unclassified screenshots are excluded from the public presentation but remain in the original archive.

Photo captions describe only what is visible. Images of on-property guest interiors are not tied to particular Airbnb cabin listings until confirmed by the owner.

Only use the originals with confirmed rights to publish; confirm photographer credits and guest permissions with Scott before public launch. Generated compressed WebP images are staged locally in `public/photos/experience`.
